THE COMPLETE FOUNDATION

Learn the game.

The goal is to beat the dealer without going over 21. You do not need to get as close to 21 as possible on every hand; the dealer's visible card changes the best decision.

LESSON 01

Card values & winning

2–10 are worth their number. J, Q and K are worth 10. Ace is worth 1 or 11, whichever helps without busting.

Hard: no Ace currently counts as 11. Soft: an Ace counts as 11 (A + 6 = soft 17). Bust: total above 21, automatic loss.

Natural blackjack: A + any 10-value card on your original two-card hand. It beats a regular 21, but ties dealer blackjack.

LESSON 02

How a round works

  1. You and the dealer each get two cards; one dealer card is face-up.
  2. If dealer shows A, insurance is offered; basic strategy says decline.
  3. Dealer peeks for blackjack with A or 10 showing. Natural blackjacks resolve immediately.
  4. You make decisions until you stand, bust, double, surrender, or finish split hands.
  5. The dealer reveals the hole card and draws to 17 or higher, standing on soft 17.
  6. The higher valid total wins. Equal totals push. Winning natural blackjack normally pays 3:2.
LESSON 03

Your five actions

HITTake another card. You can keep hitting until you stand, reach 21 or bust.
STANDKeep your total and end this hand.
DOUBLEDouble the hypothetical bet, take exactly one card, then stand. Available on any first two cards, including after a split.
SPLITIf your first two cards are the same value, make two hands. Up to four hands total. Split Aces receive one card each and cannot be re-split.
SURRENDERGive up half your hypothetical wager to end the original two-card hand, only after the dealer has checked for blackjack.
LESSON 04

THE SINGLE MOST IMPORTANT IDEA

Play your cards against their upcard.

Hard 16 against dealer 6? Stand. Hard 16 against dealer 10? Surrender under our rules. Same hand, different correct answer.

LESSON 05

Remember the core patterns

  • Hard 17+: always stand.
  • Hard 12: stand vs dealer 4–6, otherwise hit.
  • Hard 13–16: stand vs 2–6; otherwise hit unless surrender applies.
  • Hard 9: double vs 3–6. Hard 10: double vs 2–9. Hard 11: double vs 2–10.
  • Always split Aces and 8s. Never split 5s or 10-value pairs.
  • Soft 18: double vs 3–6, stand vs 2, 7–8, hit vs 9, 10, A.
  • Insurance/even money: decline in basic strategy.
LESSON 06 · OPTIONAL

Hi-Lo counting basics

Only count visible cards. The dealer's face-down card counts when revealed.

+12, 3, 4, 5, 6
07, 8, 9
−110, J, Q, K, A

Running count (RC) adds these values since the last shuffle. True count (TC) ≈ RC ÷ decks unseen. The app lets you test yourself; counting does not affect its basic-strategy scoring.

Important: basic strategy by itself does not produce a long-run player edge. Card counting never guarantees profits.
